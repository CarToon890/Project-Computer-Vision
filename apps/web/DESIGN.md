# Established OMR visual system

Inherited from web/style.css: warm off-white page, white working surface,
forest green #12675b actions, deep green #17302e text, muted #63746f,
subtle #d9e2dc rules, amber only for review warnings. Thai body text with
Tahoma/Noto Sans Thai fallback, generous line height, tabular question numbers.
Use an orderly working document rather than a marketing hero or metric cards.
44px touch targets, visible focus, wrapped navigation, one dominant action per step.
On mobile, upload and review become one column; the answer image leads the review.
Labels, counts and words accompany colors. Keep technical comparisons in details.

Surface mode: Operate. Sequence: start, review, score/export, history. The specified
workflow and green identity are already approved; extend this world, do not rebrand.
